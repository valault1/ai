# Final Verdict — Meta Platforms, Inc. (META)

## Verdict: 🟡 WATCHLIST

### Elevator Pitch
Meta owns the infrastructure of social connection through Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp. While it's spending billions to lead in artificial intelligence, its core advertising business remains a cash machine that generated over $60 billion in net income last year. It is a fantastic business at a slightly high price; wait for a market dip to secure a better margin of safety.

### Scores
- Business Quality:       9/10
- Financial Strength:     9/10
- Moat Durability:        9/10
- Valuation Attractiveness: 6/10
- **Overall:**            8/10

### Reasoning
Meta is a dominant platform with an exceptional network effect and world-class operating margins. However, it currently trades at a 20% premium to its historical P/E average and its free cash flow yield is low (1.4%) as it pours capital into AI data centers. It's a top-tier business, but not currently at an attractive entry price.

### Target Entry Note
A price below $580 (closer to the 5-year average 24x P/E) would offer a significantly better margin of safety.

### Summary
- Quant: BORDERLINE (2/4 Big 4)
- Moat: Network Effect, Wide
- Debt Risk: Low
- Valuation: Premium
- Top Risk: Regulatory & Privacy Headwinds
