# Final Verdict — Alphabet Inc. (GOOG)

## Verdict: MORE RESEARCH

### Elevator Pitch
Alphabet is an advertising and cloud computing behemoth with an incredibly deep moat around its core search business. While it has an otherwise pristine balance sheet, creeping regulatory threats and AI disruption present substantial long-term risks. Furthermore, its current 31x P/E valuation combined with a low 0.9% FCF yield demands further patience and deeper research before buying.

### Scores
- Business Quality:       9/10
- Financial Strength:     8/10
- Moat Durability:        8/10
- Valuation Attractiveness: 4/10
- **Overall:**            7/10

### Reasoning
While GOOG passed on Operating Margin and ROE, it failed on valuation (P/E) and FCF Yield in our screen. The business is fundamentally exceptional but priced for perfection. Therefore, more research is needed to determine whether the AI threat is structural or merely a short-term noise.

### Target Entry Note
Would become a WATCHLIST/STRONG BUY candidate at a P/E < 22x or during severe macro selloffs.

### Summary
- Quant: PROCEED (2/4 Big 4)
- Moat: Multiple, Wide
- Debt Risk: Low
- Valuation: Premium
- Top Risk: Regulatory and Antitrust scrutiny
